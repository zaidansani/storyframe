# Executive Summary

Uptime across all regions held at **99.97%** this quarter, with one notable
incident in the `us-east-1` cluster caused by a misconfigured autoscaling
policy. The trend line below tells the real story: response times crept up
through May as traffic grew faster than our scaling policy could react, then
dropped sharply once the fix shipped in early June.

rolled out to every internal service, engineers stopped batching changes to

incidents.

# What Changed

1. Migrated the autoscaler from a fixed threshold to a predictive model.
2. Added circuit breakers to the payments service.
3. Rolled out canary deploys to all internal services.

The autoscaler fix was the highest-leverage change. Here's the relevant
snippet from the new scaling policy:

```yaml
autoscaling:
    min_replicas: 4
    max_replicas: 40
    target_cpu_percent: 65
    scale_down_cooldown: 300s
```

Deploy duration held steady even as replica counts scaled up under the new
policy — a good sign the predictive model isn't just reacting faster, it's
scaling more precisely.

- Should we extend circuit breakers to the notifications service?
- Is 40 max replicas enough headroom for the holiday traffic spike?
- Do we need a dedicated on-call rotation for the autoscaler?
